## LUCiFEx

The goal of LUCiFEx was to bring a greater amount of flexibility and convenience for solving time-dependent problems with FEniCS. In particular, I wanted finite difference discretizations in time to not be hard-coded but abstracted out by the implementation of finite difference operators acting on time-dependent quantities.
